# Getting help

- Read the manual with `tstamp man`, or open `docs/manual.txt`.
- Look at `tstamp examples` for ready-to-use commands.
- Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue and pick a template.
- Security problem? Follow `SECURITY.md` instead of opening an issue.

When you ask for help, include the output of `tstamp version` and
`tstamp status`, plus your shell and operating system versions.

TStamp logs hold the full text of your commands. Check any log you share
for passwords and tokens first.

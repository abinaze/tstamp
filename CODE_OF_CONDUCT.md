# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free place for everyone,
whatever their background, experience level or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused, out of respect for everyone's time.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and
code reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests,
and may block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the
maintainer through the profile at https://github.com/abinaze. Reports are
handled privately.

This code of conduct is written in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
